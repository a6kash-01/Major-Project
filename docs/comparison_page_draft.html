Measuring

How it was measured

  • The published project was run from its original code, unmodified, on the same machine and data.
  • Speaker separation: speaker labels are removed from conversations whose true speakers are known, and the share of words given back to the right speaker is counted.
  • ROUGE-1 / 2 / L: word, word-pair and longest-sequence overlap between the generated summary and a reference summary.
  • Facts kept: the numbers (doses, readings, durations, ages) and drug names in the reference that also appear in the summary.
  • Word error rate: words substituted, missed or added by Whisper, divided by the words actually spoken.

Limits to state

  • The speaker rules were developed while looking at the 10-case benchmark, so its 96.1% is optimistic. The 88.4% on unseen MTS-Dialog is the fair figure.
  • The benchmark has only 10 cases, written by the published project's authors.
  • Fact grounding raises ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2 and facts kept, but lowers ROUGE-L slightly for the best model (0.312 to 0.303).
  • Synthetic voices are clearer than real clinic recordings, so real word error rates will be higher for every model.
  • Speaker separation works from text only; it does not yet use the voices.
